> [!NOTE]
> Opportunity is more than an available resource. Check whether the rules, support, and follow-through let people use it.

> [!quote] Field Note
> A resource creates opportunity only when the surrounding system helps people use it.

> [!summary] The Reader Promise
> Learn to identify barriers, owners, and measures that show whether people can use an opportunity.

## The Field Note

> [!example] Practical frame
> Name the barrier, who can change it, and what result would count as progress.

An opportunity can exist on paper and still be hard to use. A job may require credentials that do not match its tasks. A family may need childcare or transport before a parent can join training. A public institution may promise evidence-based decisions without showing how it will measure results.

Recent examples point to these different parts of the problem. The Kauffman Foundation describes projects that will test employer hiring practices and help families navigate barriers such as childcare and transportation. The World Bank Group has named a chief economist to lead its research and data agenda. Pew reports sharply different party views about whether press freedom has declined.

These sources describe plans, an institutional appointment, and survey answers. They do not prove that a funded project has improved employment, that a new appointment will change development outcomes, or that survey responses settle the meaning of press freedom. Treat each claim according to what it can show.

The practical move is to map the system around the resource. Name the barrier, identify who can change it, and choose a measure that would reveal whether access improved. Then check what people experience and what the institution does over time.

When an opportunity involves moving from a venture into employment, make the relevant work visible. Translate experience into role-specific evidence without assuming every employer will interpret a founder title the same way.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> List the steps and resources needed to take part in an opportunity. Which step could keep someone out?

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Compare hiring requirements with the work a role actually involves. Track whether clearer requirements change who can advance.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Ask learners what practical barriers affect participation. Identify which partner can address each one.

## Reading Room

[Asanov, Mavlikeeva (2026). Is Self-Employment a Career Trap? A Large-Scale Field Experiment in the Labor Market Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice.](https://doi.org/10.1177/10422587261440312)

A labor-market field experiment sent more than 8,000 fictitious résumés to examine transitions from self-employment. Applicants with self-employment histories received fewer callbacks than wage earners, particularly for associate professional positions. The study concerns employer responses to applications, not eventual job performance or every labor market. A venture history should not be treated as proof of either suitability or inability. When an opportunity involves moving from a venture into employment, make the relevant work visible. Translate experience into role-specific evidence without assuming every employer will interpret a founder title the same way. This is an abstract-based reading; the suggested practice is a proposed application.
