> [!quote] Field Note
> A visible result lasts only when the enabling system can carry it.

> [!summary] The Reader Promise
> Use systems, measures, stakeholders, and revision triggers to test whether a visible result can hold up.

## The Field Note

> [!example] Practical frame
> For any result, name the enabling system, measured constraint, affected people, and evidence that would change your response.

Today's evidence moves from development finance to operating measures, labor talks, and education. Each source shows a visible result with a less visible system beneath it. Capital needs coordination and risk tools. Indicators need careful timing. Negotiations affect trust and service. Judgment needs practice and accountability.

The evidence has clear limits. Aggregate measures do not predict one firm's results. Reports of labor disputes do not settle their merits or outcomes. Institutional claims do not independently prove long-term effects. An education framework does not prove every proposed method works.
