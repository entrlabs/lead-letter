## The Field Note

> [!example] Make the operating system visible
> For one active initiative, name the responsible owner, the dependency most likely to fail, the evidence trigger, and the route for revising the plan.

The World Bank reports that Mission 300 has connected more than 50 million people to electricity across 40 African countries. The goal is to reach 300 million by 2030. AACSB argues that faculty engage more readily with accreditation when it connects to their work. That work includes teaching, learning, curriculum, and educational outcomes. Inside Higher Ed reports that proposed federal grant rules could create uncertainty about termination authority, indirect costs, and compliance planning. AP’s current Business page lists legal questions around new Canada tariffs, but the article body was not exposed in Lightpanda.

These cases support one bounded practice: make the carrying system visible before treating a goal as complete. Name who owns delivery, which dependency could break, what evidence would change the decision, and how the plan would be revised. The sources do not prove that Mission 300 will reach its goal or that accreditation guidance causes better learning. They do not prove that proposed rules will become final or that tariff headlines establish a firm-level economic effect.

## Mental Model Lens

> [!tip] Thinking Tool
> **Mental model:** [The Map Is Not the Territory](https://fs.blog/map-and-territory/)  
> **Useful idea:** Reports, policies, and metrics simplify reality; check them against the lived system they describe.  
> **Short quote:** “You need to touch the territory.”  
> **Use today:** Compare one dashboard or policy claim with a direct user, student, founder, or staff experience before treating it as complete.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Compare a stated target with the people, systems, and lived experience that would make it real.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Name the owner, fragile dependency, evidence trigger, and revision route for one active initiative.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Translate one framework or policy into the teaching, research, or student outcome it is meant to support.
