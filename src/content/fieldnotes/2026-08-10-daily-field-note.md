> [!quote] Field Note
> A gain is not fully understood until you can see who receives it and what changed.

> [!summary] The Reader Promise
> Use one distribution check to see whether an efficiency gain improves output, pay, hours, or only the headline.

## The Field Note

> [!example] Check the Gain
> Put the denominator, distribution, comparison period, and evidence limit beside the positive number.

U.S. nonfarm business productivity rose in the second quarter. Output rose faster than hours worked.

The same release showed real hourly compensation falling for the quarter. Labor share also reached the lowest point in its series.

Those facts are not a contradiction. They are different measures. Output per hour does not tell you who received the gain, how purchasing power changed, or whether workload and quality improved.

Research impact has the same trap. More papers can be real progress, but the count does not show whether a teacher, firm, policymaker, or community used the work.

This does not prove why pay, labor share, or research use changed. It gives you a safer question: what did the gain measure, who received it, and what did it leave out?

## Mental Model Lens

> [!tip] Thinking Tool
> **Mental model:** [Circle of Competence](https://fs.blog/circle-of-competence/)
> **Useful idea:** Know where the measure and your knowledge stop before confidence carries the claim farther.
> **Short quote:** "The size of that circle is not important."
> **Use today:** Mark one claim as known, estimated, or outside your current competence.

> [!note] Evidence Limit
> These measures do not establish one cause or one universal remedy. They show which questions the headline leaves open.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Which role, place, or measure sits behind the headline you are using?

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Did the gain improve output, pay, hours, quality, customer value, or only one metric?

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> What would show that a research or learning output changed what someone could do?
