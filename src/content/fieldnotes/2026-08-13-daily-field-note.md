> [!quote] Field Note
> A strong plan connects the idea, the conditions around it, and the evidence that shows whether it worked.

> [!summary] The Reader Promise
> Use a pathway check to see whether a research, policy, or founder initiative can produce a useful result after launch.

## The Field Note

> [!example] Test the Pathway, Not Just the Promise
> Before calling an initiative successful, map the intended result, the next two effects, and the conditions that make the result possible.

AACSB's 2026 research-impact framework asks business schools to look beyond publications and citations toward contributions to business, policy, education, and society. It describes impact as nonlinear and connected to scholarship, teaching, and external engagement.

The World Bank's jobs FAQ names infrastructure, clear rules, and capital as conditions that shape whether firms can grow and hire. The IMF's topic structure places technology, finance, fiscal policy, and governance in the same wider policy map.

These sources do not prove that one intervention causes better outcomes. They show why the pathway around an idea matters.

The practical move is second-order thinking. Ask what happens next, who responds, and what could become worse even if the first result looks good. Then test one consequence before scaling.

## Mental Model Lens

> [!tip] Thinking Tool
> **Mental model:** [Second-Order Thinking](https://fs.blog/second-order-thinking/)
> **Useful idea:** Ask "And then what?" to examine ripple effects and longer-term implications.
> **Short quote:** "And then what?"
> **Use today:** Write the likely effects in 10 minutes, 10 months, and 10 years before launch.

> [!note] Evidence Limit
> These sources are frameworks and guidance, not one causal test of a single intervention.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Ask what a policy or program changes after its first visible result.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Map how customers, suppliers, employees, and regulators may respond next.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Measure stakeholder use and pathway evidence alongside output counts.
