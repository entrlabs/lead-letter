## The Field Note

> [!example] Make the operating problem visible
> Name who carries the risk, who must approve the change, and what users need before designing the intervention.

The World Bank Group announced a guarantee-based initiative intended to help local financial institutions expand digital payments. The program identifies a concrete operating constraint: settlement risk can limit the institutions that can serve customers. Its estimates describe an aim, not a delivered result or equal access.

Bay Path University's completed integration of Cambridge College adds a second constraint. Inside Higher Ed reports that the process took more than two and a half years and required federal, state, and accreditor approval. Formal approval marks a milestone. It does not establish academic, financial, or student outcomes.

AACSB's case-teaching workshop points to the work inside the intervention. Practice needs planning, participation, and feedback designed around the people using it. Farnam Street's listening prompt supplies the first move: ask enough questions to hear the real problem before prescribing a fix. This is a practical discipline, not proof that listening alone improves management.

Use the sequence when an access, integration, or learning problem appears: locate the risk and approval bottlenecks, listen to the people affected, and design the next practice around the constraint that remains.

## Mental Model Lens

> [!tip] Thinking Tool
> **Mental model:** [The Power of Listening](https://fs.blog/brain-food/august-9-2026/)
> **Useful idea:** Listening can surface the problem before a leader supplies a solution.
> **Short quote:** “Many of the problems I hear don’t require me to offer solutions.”
> **Use today:** Ask two follow-up questions about one operating problem before proposing a fix.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Separate the reported milestone from the outcome that still needs evidence, then identify whose experience could clarify the gap.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Map one customer or team problem through risk, approval, use, and feedback before selecting the next intervention.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Design one case discussion around the decision learners must make, the evidence they need, and the feedback that can improve the next attempt.
