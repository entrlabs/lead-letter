## The Field Note

Small Business Saturday gives shoppers a reason to try a neighborhood store. The US Small Business Administration identifies November 28 as this year’s observance. Its figures describe the scale of independent business across the country. They cannot tell a shop owner how many Saturday customers will come back.

A busy counter creates a measurement problem. A person who came for a special offer may return next week, or this may be their only visit. Pick one practical measure now. For example, record how many customers redeem a return-visit offer during the following month. Keep the original number of offers beside the result.

> [!example] Try this
> Agree on a return window and a way to recognize a repeat visit before reviewing sales.

## Reading Room

**Paper:** Rus et al. (2025), “Intra-team learning behaviors and psychological safety: Linking team leader coaching behaviors and team performance in work teams,” Team Performance Management: an International Journal. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1108/TPM-05-2024-0052).

Based on the abstract, Rus and colleagues studied 72 Romanian work teams. Team members rated coaching, psychological safety and learning behaviors. Leaders rated performance. The authors found an indirect link between coaching and performance through how teams learned together. The cross-sectional design cannot tell us whether coaching caused the difference. Try a small version of the question after a busy weekend. Ask front-line staff what surprised them and which assumption the sales figures leave open. Record one change to test and agree on what would count as a better result.
