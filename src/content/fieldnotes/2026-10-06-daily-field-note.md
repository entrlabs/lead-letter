## The Field Note

> [!example] Practical frame
> Before scaling, name the owner, capacity constraint, local measure, and evidence that would trigger revision.

Today's sources connect education leadership, climate preparation, official monitoring, and team capacity. They show why a large plan needs local evidence and a named owner. A profile can reveal an institution but not prove outcomes. A forecast can guide attention but not establish local impact. A monitoring system can organize evidence without making the decision. A capacity case can reveal trade-offs without proving one universal method.

The practical task is to keep observation, outlook, and impact claims separate. Then make the operating choice visible. This evidence does not prove educational outcomes, a specific regional climate effect, or the causal effect of one management practice.
