## The Field Note

A confident story can hide a weak test. Pew's review of 2026 polling found that polls captured the election's direction but often understated the size of the Democratic advantage. Knowledge at Wharton describes another risk: losing a competition can strain relationships after the result. The lesson is not that forecasts or contests are useless. It is that the next consequence needs a place in the plan.

The same discipline helps with harder-to-read signals. A federal trade release can show a monthly change, a moving average, and a year-to-date comparison at the same time. A childcare model can show how families, employers, philanthropy, and public entities share a funding problem. These sources describe different evidence types. They do not prove one universal rule or predict what a particular organization will experience.

> [!example] Try this
> Before one consequential decision, write the expected outcome, the main failure mode, the accountable owner, the evidence needed next, and the review date.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> State what you expect to happen, how large the error could be, and what observation would change your view.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Add the post-result relationship and the review date to the decision record, especially when people compete for the same outcome.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Ask learners to label whether a source reports an aggregate measure, a program description, a research discussion, or an outcome evaluation.
