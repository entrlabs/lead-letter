## The Field Note

Getting the direction right is not the same as estimating the size correctly. Pew’s early polling review compares predictions with unofficial results in 27 races. It reports a mean signed difference of about four percentage points.

The counts were provisional. The analysis does not establish why the errors occurred. That uncertainty is part of the finding, not a reason to invent an explanation.

Apply the distinction to your next project. Write what should change, by how much, and by when. State what you will observe and what would count against your explanation. If the evidence is incomplete, keep the judgment open rather than rewriting the prediction after the event.

> [!example] Try this
> Write one project forecast, its review date, and one observation that would make you revise it. Save the dated version before acting.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Record what you expect to learn before starting an activity, then compare it with what you can demonstrate afterward.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Separate a project’s direction of change from the size and timing you promised.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Ask learners to distinguish a change in confidence from a change in demonstrated practice.

## Reading Room

**[Thinking and doing differently: the immediate impact of entrepreneurship education on STEM PhD students](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10961-026-10339-6); Jenkins et al., 2026, *Journal of Technology Transfer*.**

*Based on the abstract. Abstract available; full-text access not verified.*

A qualitative study of entrepreneurship education examined biotechnology and biomedical researchers in an industry-linked program. It distinguished changes in how participants understood their industry from changes in what they did during research. The findings are preliminary and do not establish that the program caused better commercial outcomes. They nevertheless suggest a sharper evaluation question than whether participants enjoyed a course. Ask for a specific practice that changed, such as a revised experiment or a new industry conversation. Then check whether that change persisted. An immediate action and a long-term venture result need different measures.
