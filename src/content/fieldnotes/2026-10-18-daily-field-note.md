> [!quote] Field Note
> An opportunity becomes useful only when the system around it carries people through the next step.

> [!summary] The Reader Promise
> Use four current examples to find where use or delivery breaks, then measure the support needed to continue.

## The Field Note

> [!example] Practical frame
> Before adding a new plan, name where the path could fail, assign an owner, and choose a measure that shows whether people can go on.

The World Bank's jobs speech connects finance to basic systems, clear rules, skills, and small-business capacity. Kauffman's Project Choice reflection shows that a scholarship promise still needed wider support around students. AACSB frames research impact as a path through business, policy, education, and society. UN News asks whether a global nature plan is moving from promise into use.

These are distinct examples, not proof of one universal formula. Together they support a practical test: find where use or delivery breaks, then improve the system around it before widening the plan. Measure staying power, useful work, use, or delivery, not access or promise alone.

**Evidence limit:** These sources show distinct examples. They do not set one universal model or prove that any single plan will produce a set result.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Trace one chance from entry to finish and name the support that would help you go on when the first barrier appears.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Name the key support needed before growing the next step, then assign an owner and a review measure.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Pair access measures with staying power, use, and delivery measures so the path can improve where it breaks.
