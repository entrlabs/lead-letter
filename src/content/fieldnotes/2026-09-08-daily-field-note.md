## The Field Note

> [!example] The system must meet the person
> Name the formal map, identify the person or process it is meant to serve, and collect one observable check before claiming success.

The World Bank Group's appointment of an Auditor General establishes an independent assurance role for governance, risk management, and internal controls. It supports a lesson about who can inspect a system. It does not prove that every control works or that trust has improved.

The same distinction appears in teaching, housing access, and founder development. AACSB lists a workshop on case teaching. That listing shows professional attention to designed practice, but not improved learning. For housing access, compare available units with eligibility, affordability, and the steps applicants must complete. Empty space alone cannot explain why someone lacks a home. Farnam Street presents Steve Jobs's time away from Apple as a leadership story shaped by focus, trust, compromise, and discipline. That account is a practitioner case, not a general law about failure.

Together, these sources support a practical move: test the map against lived conditions. Ask what evidence would show that assurance reaches a control, that a teaching routine changes a decision, that a housing service reaches an eligible person, or that a setback changed a leader's behavior. The evidence does not establish that any one system works simply because its formal structure or story is persuasive.

Inspect a support process from the recipient’s side. Ask which step adds strain, who explains the options, and whether the person can use the policy when they need it.

## Mental Model Lens

> [!tip] Thinking Tool
> **Mental model:** [The Map Is Not the Territory](https://fs.blog/map-and-territory/)
> **Useful idea:** A policy, metric, or leadership story is a map. Check it against the people and conditions it describes.
> **Short quote:** “You need to touch the territory.”
> **Use today:** Compare one formal promise with one observable experience before deciding that the system works.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Choose one institutional claim and label the evidence that is observed, reported, uncertain, or assumed.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Identify the person a control, service, or leadership practice is meant to help, then collect one direct check from that person's experience.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Ask learners to compare a case or model with an observable decision and name the missing condition that could change the recommendation.

## Reading Room

[Barnes, Lacerenza, Hardin (2026). How policy implementation impacts employee suffering at work: An exploration of medical leaves of absence. Journal of Applied Psychology.](https://doi.org/10.1037/apl0001383)

A qualitative study drew on 48 leave-takers and leave coordinators to examine medical leave. It argues that implementing a policy should address suffering and healing, beyond balancing work and life. This is an account of experiences and a proposed framework, not a trial of every recommended policy. Its workplace setting also differs from student support. Inspect a support process from the recipient’s side. Ask which step adds strain, who explains the options, and whether the person can use the policy when they need it. This is an abstract-based reading; the suggested practice is a proposed application.
