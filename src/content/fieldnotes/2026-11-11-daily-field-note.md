> [!quote] Field Note
> A bigger promise needs a clearer control system behind it.

> [!summary] The Reader Promise
> Before expanding a product, program, or institution, identify the control, owner, and evidence that must grow with the promise.

## The Field Note

MIT Sloan describes four places where companies need AI guardrails: project selection, workforce enablement, supplier governance, and the move into production. AACSB's accreditation announcement describes a multi-year process of internal assessment, mentorship, and peer review. The Bureau of Labor Statistics reports that October payroll employment rose by 165,000 while unemployment changed little at 4.1 percent. The World Bank's Philippines report connects modeled growth and job gains to productivity, connectivity, human capital, regulation, and technology adoption.

These sources describe management guidance, an accreditation process, national labor context, and a modeled reform scenario. They do not prove that a particular rollout, accreditation, job-search strategy, or reform package will succeed.

Before you approve expansion, name the failure mode the system must prevent, the person or team who owns that control, and the measure that would show whether it is working.

> [!example] Try this
> Write three lines before approving scale: the failure mode to prevent, the owner of the control, and the evidence that would show the control is working.

## Mental Model Lens

> [!tip] Thinking Tool
> **Mental model:** [The Price of Admission](https://fs.blog/brain-food/october-25-2026/)
> **Useful idea:** Progress can require accepting a visible period of imperfect performance while the system learns.
> **Short quote:** “If you don’t know what will work, eliminate what won’t.”
> **Use today:** Remove one foreseeable failure mode before demanding more speed or scale.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Treat national labor data as context, then check the occupation, place, and skills that matter for your decision.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Assign an owner for production, supplier, cost, and workforce risk before adding a new system or workflow.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Separate accreditation process evidence from evidence about learner or employer outcomes.

## Reading Room

**Paper:** Shourkaei and Dyck (2025), *Organization*. [Scaling sustainability in businesses with a post-growth orientation](https://doi.org/10.1177/13505084251350847).

Based on the abstract, this exploratory study examined 35 small and medium-sized businesses with a post-growth orientation. The authors identified four approaches to scaling positive social and ecological impact: enhancement, expansion, bridging, and collaboration. That gives teams a useful question before adding size: what kind of impact are we trying to scale? The abstract does not establish which approach works best or prove effects on profits. Its selected businesses also limit transfer to firms pursuing rapid growth. Try comparing a larger operation with a partnership that serves the same need. Name the capacity each option requires, then choose one outcome to track.
