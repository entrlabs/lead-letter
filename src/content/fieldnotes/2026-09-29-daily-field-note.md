## The Field Note

> [!example] Practical frame
> Before acting on evidence, name the decision owner, the coordination routine, the feedback measure, and the revision trigger.

The Census business-application indicator is useful, but it is not a count of completed firms. AACSB frames business education around judgment when information is incomplete. The World Bank presents research leadership as a path from evidence toward use. Gallup’s team research summary points to routines that connect individual strengths with shared work.

These sources document different measures, arguments, choices, and team findings. They do not prove one mechanism explains every domain, that teaching practices cause judgment, or that future research leadership will produce a specific outcome. They support one practical test: evidence becomes useful when the working practice makes judgment, coordination, feedback, and result visible.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> Take one claim and name the judgment it requires, the missing information, and the evidence that would change your view.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Make the decision owner, coordination routine, feedback measure, and revision trigger explicit before scaling a plan.

> Assess whether learners can connect evidence to judgment, adaptation, and consequences rather than only repeat the evidence.
