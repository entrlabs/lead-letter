## The Field Note

> [!example] Check the Next Consequence
> Before you trust a signal, ask what consequence it creates next.

A forecast is not a plan.

A jobs target is not a job.

An engagement score is not a manager routine.

An impact report is not proof that someone made a better decision.

Each of those signals may still matter. The mistake is stopping too early.

The World Bank's global outlook points to slower growth and risks from energy, debt, inflation, and geopolitical strain. Its jobs work says more than 1 billion young people in developing countries will reach working age over the next 10 to 15 years.

Gallup's workplace report page says only 20 percent of employees worldwide were engaged in 2025. AACSB and Inside Higher Ed show business schools facing pressure to connect research, learning, careers, governance, and impact.

The useful move is to ask the second question: and then what?

If growth slows, what buffer protects the people served by the organization?

If a jobs strategy names youth employment, what firm conditions make hiring possible?

If engagement is low, what manager behavior changes tomorrow?

If a school claims impact, what path connects research or teaching to a visible outcome?

This does not prove one answer works everywhere. It does give leaders a better first habit. Do not treat the signal as the result. Test the next consequence before you act.

## Mental Model Lens

> [!tip] Thinking Tool: Second-Order Thinking
> **Mental model:** [Second-Order Thinking](https://fs.blog/second-order-thinking/)  
> **Useful idea:** The first effect is rarely the whole story.  
> **Short quote:** "And then what?"  
> **Use today:** Before acting on a signal, write the next consequence it may create.

> [!note] Evidence Limit
> These sources do not prove one universal sequence, intervention, or impact model. They show why leaders should test what a signal asks people to do next.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> What proof, habit, or support turns one career signal into a real opportunity?

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> What changes after the metric moves, and who has to carry that change?

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Where does a research, teaching, or advising claim need a clearer evidence path?
