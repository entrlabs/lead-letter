> [!quote] Field Note
> A useful promise tells people what to build, what to test, and where the edge ends.

> [!summary] The Reader Promise
> Use one test to turn a broad promise into evidence-backed action.

## The Field Note

> [!example] Test the Promise
> Before you make a promise, name the evidence, the next test, and the limit.

Today's sources all pointed to the same habit.

A climate target helped suppliers see future demand. A community group could see local climate risk, but still needed staff and money to act. A retail number helped managers read demand, but only if they kept the error band in view. A small factory helped students learn because they worked on a real system. A product line worked only when the choices helped customers decide. A founder story helped students when it showed the hard steps, not only the happy ending.

The useful promise is not the loudest one. It is the one another person can test.

Try this three-part check:

1. **Evidence:** What do we know from the source, customer, class, or community?
2. **Next test:** What would show that the promise is working?
3. **Boundary:** Where does our knowledge stop?

This does not prove that every target, program, class, product line, or founder story will work. It does show a better way to speak before people depend on your words: make the promise specific enough to test.

## Mental Model Lens

> [!tip] Thinking Tool: Circle of Competence
> **Mental model:** [Circle of Competence](https://fs.blog/circle-of-competence/)  
> **Useful idea:** Know where your evidence is strong, and name the point where other people need to test it.  
> **Short quote:** "I stay around those spots."  
> **Use today:** Write what you know, what you can test, and what someone closer to the work should verify.

> [!note] Evidence Limit
> These sources do not show that one target, community program, market reading, class model, product-line choice, or founder-story course works everywhere.

## Use It Today

> [!question] For Students
> When you hear a big claim, ask what small test would show whether it is real.

> [!question] For Managers And Founders
> Before announcing a target, product plan, or offer, write the partner signal it is meant to create.

> [!question] For Educators And Advisors
> Ask learners to separate the promise, the proof, and the next test in any case discussion.
